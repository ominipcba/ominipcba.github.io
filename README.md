OminiPCB

Engineering resources for PCB design, PCB manufacturing, and PCBA.

OminiPCB provides practical calculators, technical references, and manufacturing guidance for engineers working with printed circuit boards and electronics manufacturing. It helps hardware teams evaluate design constraints, prepare RFQ data, and reduce fabrication, assembly, sourcing, and quality risks before production.

PCB Engineering Tools

* PCB Trace Width Calculator — Calculate PCB trace width based on current, copper thickness, and temperature rise.
* PCB Impedance Calculator — Estimate controlled impedance for common PCB transmission-line structures.
* PCB Stackup Calculator — Explore PCB layer stackups, board thickness, copper weight, dielectric spacing, and key electrical parameters.
* BOM → PCBA Quote — Upload a BOM and prepare an initial PCBA quotation request for engineering review.

About OminiPCB

OminiPCB is operated by Omini Electronics Limited, providing PCB manufacturing, PCBA, EMS, and component sourcing support for electronics companies and engineering teams.

The company supports prototype and low-to-medium volume production, with a focus on practical engineering review, manufacturability, sourcing risk, inspection, and production handoff.
